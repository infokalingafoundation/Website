Kalinga Research & Technology Centre (KRTC)

The primary research & technology institute operating under the direct governance and statutory compliance of KRTF.

Governance & Parent Overview

About KRTC (krtcodisha.com)

Established in 2016 as the flagship initiative of KRTF, KRTC delivers professional technology programs, research laboratories, and skill development frameworks in Odisha.

As the governing parent body, KRTF provides KRTC with:

  • Statutory & Legal Coverage: Operating under KRTF's MCA Govt. of India registration & Udyam MSME accreditation.
  • Quality Frameworks: Audited under KRTF's ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems.
  • Policy Governance: Strategic directional control, academic policy oversight, and ethical guidelines.
  • Industry Alliances: Channeling corporate partnerships and institutional funding to KRTC programs.
