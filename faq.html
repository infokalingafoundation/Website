Frequently Asked Questions

Direct institutional answers regarding KRTF's governance, legal status, and administrative operations.

Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) is an apex registered governing foundation body established in 2016 under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Govt. of India. It provides institutional governance, quality management, and administrative policy oversight for technology and research initiatives in Odisha.
Yes. KRTF is formally registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Govt. of India. It is also Udyam MSME Registered under the Ministry of MSME, ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality management systems, and officially recognized by the Government of Odisha.
KRTF is the governing parent body of KRTC (Kalinga Research and Technology Centre — krtcodisha.com). The Foundation sets operational and academic policies, ensures quality compliance under ISO standards, channels research grants, and manages corporate partnerships for KRTC.
