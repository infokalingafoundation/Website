Governance Framework

Why Accreditations Matter for Our Governed Entities

Statutory certifications and ISO quality management systems ensure that all institutions governed under KRTF — including Kalinga Research and Technology Centre (KRTC — krtcodisha.com) — operate with:

  • Standardized institutional policies and administrative procedures.
  • Continuous external and internal compliance audits.
  • Official recognition for student certifications, research grants, and industry alliances.
  • Complete fiscal, statutory, and operational accountability.

Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) is a registered governing foundation body overseeing technology and research initiatives in Odisha since 2016. Governs KRTC (krtcodisha.com).
