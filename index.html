Institutional Overview

Apex Governing Body for Technology & Research

"Established in 2016, Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) operates as an independent, registered non-profit governance entity dedicated to institutional excellence, policy direction, and strategic oversight."

As the parent governing body of Kalinga Research and Technology Centre (KRTC — krtcodisha.com), KRTF ensures high standards of research compliance, educational governance, and operational integrity.
