Who We Are

The Governing Body Behind Odisha's Research & Education Movement

Founded in 2016, Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) is the registered governing parent body of KRTC, overseeing research, education, and technology initiatives across Odisha. KRTF is ISO 9001:2015 certified, MCA registered, Udyam registered, and recognized by the Government of Odisha.

As the parent body of Kalinga Research and Technology Centre (KRTC), the Foundation sets policy, ensures academic quality, and channels funding and industry partnerships into programs that prepare learners for real careers in research and technology.

  • ✅ Governing Body of KRTC
  • ✅ Policy & Academic Oversight
  • ✅ Research Funding & Support
  • ✅ Industry Partnership Management
  • ✅ Certification & Quality Assurance

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions about KRTF

Clear, direct answers about the Foundation, its structure, recognition, and how to engage with it.

Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) is a registered governing organization established in 2016 under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Govt. of India. It oversees and funds research, education, and technology initiatives across Odisha, primarily through its flagship institute, KRTC. KRTF is ISO 9001:2015 certified and recognized by the Government of Odisha.
KRTF (Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation) is the governing parent body of KRTC (Kalinga Research and Technology Centre). The Foundation sets academic policy, ensures quality standards, channels funding, and manages industry partnerships for all programs run through KRTC.
Yes. KRTF is formally registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Govt. of India. It is also Udyam Registered under the Ministry of MSME, ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality management systems, and officially recognized by the Government of Odisha for its role in education and technology development.
Under KRTF's governance, KRTC offers 12+ programs across three levels: Diploma, PG Diploma, and Masters. Key disciplines include Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, Digital Marketing, Computer Applications, Research Methodology, and Technology Management — all 1-year programs designed for career-ready outcomes.
