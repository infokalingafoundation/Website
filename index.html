Who We Are

A Registered Foundation Governing Research & Technology in Odisha

Founded in 2016, Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) is a registered governing body under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Govt. of India — providing institutional oversight, policy direction, and governance to research and technology initiatives across Odisha.

The Foundation is ISO 9001:2015 certified, Udyam Registered under the Ministry of MSME, and formally recognized by the Government of Odisha. As the governing parent body, KRTF manages KRTC (Kalinga Research and Technology Centre, krtcodisha.com), ensuring quality, accountability, and long-term impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers about the Foundation, its registration, governance, and how to reach us.

Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) is a registered governing organization established in 2016 under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Govt. of India. It provides institutional oversight and governance to research and technology initiatives in Odisha, and is the governing parent body of KRTC (krtcodisha.com). KRTF is ISO 9001:2015 certified and recognized by the Government of Odisha.
Yes. KRTF is formally registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Govt. of India. It is also Udyam Registered under the Ministry of MSME, ISO 9001:2015 certified, and officially recognized by the Government of Odisha for its role in research and technology governance.
KRTF is the governing parent body of KRTC — Kalinga Research and Technology Centre (krtcodisha.com). The Foundation sets academic and operational policy, ensures institutional quality standards, channels research funding, and manages strategic partnerships for KRTC.
