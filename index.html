Who We Are

A Registered Governing Foundation for Research & Technology in Odisha

Founded in 2016, Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) is a registered governing body under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Govt. of India. It is ISO 9001:2015 certified, Udyam registered, and recognized by the Government of Odisha for its governance of research and technology in the region.

The Foundation provides institutional oversight, policy direction, and strategic governance to research and technology initiatives across Odisha — ensuring quality, accountability, and long-term impact.

Our Primary Initiative

Kalinga Research and Technology Centre

As the governing parent body, KRTF provides KRTC with institutional oversight, quality assurance, policy direction, and industry partnership support — enabling KRTC to deliver impactful programs and research outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions about KRTF

Direct answers about the Foundation, its structure, legal standing, and how to reach us.

Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) is a registered governing organization established in 2016 under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Govt. of India. It provides institutional oversight, policy governance, and strategic direction for research and technology initiatives across Odisha. It is ISO 9001:2015 certified and recognized by the Government of Odisha.
Yes. KRTF is formally registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Govt. of India. It is also Udyam Registered under the Ministry of MSME, ISO 9001:2015 certified for quality management systems, and officially recognized by the Government of Odisha.
KRTF is the governing parent body of KRTC (Kalinga Research and Technology Centre). The Foundation sets policy, ensures institutional quality standards, channels research funding, and manages strategic partnerships for KRTC's operations.
You can reach KRTF by email at krtcodisha@gmail.com or by phone at +91 9556359507. The Foundation accepts enquiries for institutional collaborations, research partnerships, and governance-related matters.
