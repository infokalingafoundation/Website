Our Institutional Profile

Governing Technology & Academic Excellence Since 2016

"Kalinga Research and Technology Foundation (KRTF) was constituted to serve as an independent governing body — ensuring quality assurance, institutional governance, and policy compliance for technology institutes and research programs in Odisha."

Over the past decade, KRTF has provided strategic leadership and oversight to its primary initiative — Kalinga Research and Technology Centre (KRTC — krtcodisha.com). Through rigorous adherence to ISO 9001:2015 standards and statutory regulations under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, KRTF bridges industry demands with institutional compliance.

Primary Governance Directives

The Foundation exercises governance across four main pillars:

🏛️ Institutional Oversight
Directing operational policies, administrative compliance, and organizational integrity for governed bodies.
📜 Quality & Compliance
Enforcing ISO 9001:2015 quality frameworks and government standards across all initiatives.
🔬 Research Channeling
Allocating resources, securing strategic research grants, and fostering academic research in Odisha.
🤝 Industry & State Alliances
Fostering partnerships between government bodies, industrial partners, and educational centers.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To govern, support, and scale high-impact technology and research institutions in Odisha through transparent governance, compliance, and quality frameworks.

Our Vision

To establish Odisha as a nationally recognized hub for technology innovation and compliant academic research backed by institutional governance.
